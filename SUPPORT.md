# Support

ScacchiForge is an early-stage research project. Support happens in the open,
on GitHub, and is best effort.

- **Wrong results, slowdowns and research proposals:** open an
  [issue](https://github.com/gpicchiarelli/ScacchiForge/issues/new/choose)
  with the matching form.
- **Security reports:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public
  issue.
- **Contributing:** see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Before asking for help, include:

- the command you ran;
- the exact output;
- your operating system, CPU and the output of `sbcl --version`;
- the commit hash;
- whether `make check` passes.

For a wrong result, add the FEN and say whether the reference engine agrees.

For a performance report, add the command that produces the figures and the
numbers before and after. A figure is a measurement of one machine.
